How I think about property

Three questions before you buy

Liquidity

What are you locking up?

A house is a large, illiquid, leveraged position. Know what that capital could be doing instead.

Timing

Is now the right time for you?

Buying before income, career, and lifestyle stabilize is the most common and costly mistake.

Lifestyle finance

Does it serve your life?

Property should fit the life you are building, not a social script about when to “settle down.”
